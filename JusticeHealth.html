The study

The first program of its kind in the country.

Through the Rutgers Honors College and i.d.e.a. partnership with University Correctional Healthcare, six undergraduate researchers spent a summer alongside clinicians inside New Jersey's correctional system — then turned what they learned into a multi-domain study of inmate health.

Correctional medicine sits at the intersection of the social and economic challenges facing the country. Many people inside come from low-income backgrounds, live with substance use disorders, and carry untreated mental illness. This report asks a direct question: how well does the system care for them, and where does that care fall short?

The core finding

The most consistent problem isn't a single failure — it's one of cadence and continuity. Care shows up at intake and in crises; the intervals in between, and the return home, are where it erodes.

— Justice Health Research Team
